Short version: **some links pay us, all of them are marked, and none of them buy a recommendation.**

## How this site is funded

DevTool Digest earns through affiliate links — when you sign up for a paid tool through one of
our links, the vendor pays us a referral fee. You pay the same price either way.

We do not run display advertising. We do not sell sponsored posts. Nobody pays to be reviewed,
and nobody sees a draft before it publishes.

## How you can tell

Every paid link is marked three ways:

- a **disclosure note** appears immediately before the first paid link in an article — not buried
  in a footer you'd scroll past
- the link carries `rel="sponsored"`, so machines can see it too
- a small **¶** follows the link text

If a link has none of those, it earns us nothing.

## The rules we hold ourselves to

1. **We test before we recommend.** If we haven't run a tool, the article says so in the first
   paragraph. Look for the "researched, not tested" note.
2. **A commission never changes a verdict.** We recommend against paying for things regularly —
   including things we have affiliate relationships with — when a free or self-hosted option wins.
3. **We disclose the incentive, not just the existence.** Recurring commissions bias a writer
   differently than one-time bounties, so we tell you which kind it is.
4. **No link without a reason.** We don't sprinkle affiliate links into unrelated articles.
